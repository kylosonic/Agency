# Marketing Launch Kit

Use this as the source-of-truth copy pack for launch week campaigns.

> **Truth standard (B1–B3, B24):** Every claim in this kit must be true and provable today. There is no
> client proof layer yet — no case studies, no portfolio, no performance statistics, no fabricated
> metrics. The founding-client pilot program (below) is the only sanctioned mechanism that *builds*
> the proof layer. Nothing in this kit may present pilot terms or future work as existing proof.

## Positioning

- Brand: NovaTech AI
- Category: AI-first automation consultancy with full product engineering delivery
- Core promise: contract-first, founder-delivered agentic automation for operationally heavy Ethiopian businesses
- Primary market: growth-stage teams and operationally heavy businesses in Ethiopia and regional markets
- Pricing basis: **ETB only**, anchored starting prices ("from X ETB") — international engagements are quoted individually

## Positioning Pillars

### 1) Contract-First, Founder-Delivered — "We don't disappear"

- Headline angle: written SOW, milestone payments, named founder-owner
- Proof angle: the written scope and payment schedule exist before any work starts; the founder's name is on the contract and on the delivery
- Why it matters: agencies that vanish are the norm; a signed scope with staged payments means both sides know exactly what happens next

### 2) Payment-Integration Reliability as a Specialty

- Headline angle: Telebirr, Chapa, and CBE integrations that actually settle — not integrations that "look done"
- Proof angle: settlement verification and reconciliation are built into the scope of every payment-integration build
- Why it matters: payment integrations fail at settlement, not at the checkout screen; reconciliation built in means money flows are traceable end to end

### 3) Published, Anchored ETB Prices

- Headline angle: the only shop in the market with transparent "from" prices in ETB
- Proof angle: the pricing page itself — fixed entry offer and anchored starting prices per tier, no open-ended ranges
- Why it matters: no "contact us for pricing" stall; a visitor knows the floor before the first call

### 4) Amharic-First, SLA-Backed Maintenance Retainers

- Headline angle: maintenance with a written response-time commitment, in Amharic first
- Proof angle: every retainer lists what it includes (hosting, model usage, monitoring, defined support hours, response-time commitment)
- Why it matters: support that responds in writing, in the language your team works in

## Offer Ladder

- **Entry:** Workflow Audit — 85,000 ETB fixed, delivered within 5 business days, full fee credited toward any implementation project that follows
- **Core:** Custom agentic automation — from 250,000 / 750,000 / 1,500,000+ ETB, each tier with published scope that raises the price above the starting point
- **Expansion:** Web / mobile / SaaS product engineering — web from 45,000 / 150,000 / 250,000 ETB; mobile from 200,000 / 400,000 ETB; SaaS via waitlist ("in development — tell us you want it") until a real product exists
- **Conversion assists:** Instant Quote Wizard, Discovery Call booking, and the founding-client pilot program
- **Founding-client pilot program:** the first three implementation clients receive 15–20% off published prices, visibly framed as founding-client rates, in exchange for case study rights, a testimonial, and referral permission. The pilot is the mechanism that *builds* the proof layer — it is not presented as existing proof.

## Pricing & Payment Notes (for all copy)

- All prices are published in ETB only. There is no USD column; international engagements are described as "quoted individually."
- Payment is by bank transfer (CBE Birr) only until the business license is secured. Telebirr, Chapa, and card payments are added *after* licensing — never claimed before they exist.
- Development projects follow milestone billing (40% at kickoff, 30% at design approval, 30% before deployment); the client owns all custom work after final payment.
- Paid advertising services are not offered at launch.
