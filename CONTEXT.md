## What this is

NovaTech is a digital engineering agency in Addis Ababa (novatech.et) selling AI automation, web,
mobile, and SaaS builds to Ethiopian businesses — with published ETB prices, contract-first
positioning, and a founding-client pilot. The site is trilingual: English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo.

## Glossary

- **Workflow Audit** — the fixed entry offer: 85,000 ETB, delivered in 5 business days, full fee
  credited toward any implementation project that follows.
- **Implementation tiers** — AI automation from 250k / 750k / 1.5M+ ETB; web from 45k / 150k /
  250k; mobile from 200k / 400k. Anchored starting prices ("from X ETB"), never open ranges.
- **Support & maintenance** — AI support retainers 30–70k/month; maintenance 5–15k/month, both
  with written commitments (SLA).
- **Milestone billing** — 40% at kickoff, 30% at design approval, 30% before deployment. Full
  ownership transfers to the client after final payment.
- **Founding-client pilot** — the first three implementation clients receive 15–20% off in
  exchange for case study rights, a testimonial, and referral permission.
- **Instant quote wizard** — the site's interactive estimator; must always match the pricing page
  (same numbers, same options, never below the entry offer).
- **Discovery call** — the booked first conversation; SLA: response within 1 business day, call
  within 3, proposal within 5 of discovery.
- **SLA (published)** — the response commitments the site states: 1 / 3 / 5 business days.
- **Truth rules** — no number, story, testimonial, or stat appears without a real source; no
  portfolio until real client work exists; any demo must be a real running system.
- **Three languages** — English, Amharic (Ethiopic script), Afaan Oromo (Latin script). Amharic
  uses dedicated Ethiopic typefaces; numbers render identically across all three.
- **Visual system (Phase 7)** — the light-first Fortune-500 system: near-white surfaces, exactly
  two near-black bands per page (after the services index; pre-footer CTA), one dual-mode accent
  (bronze on light / gold on dark), typography-led heroes, services as a ruled editorial index,
  zero imagery (no photos, illustrations, or generated art), calm motion, grain texture.
- **Signature elements** — the recurring human-owned details: Amharic serif Ethiopic display
  type, monospaced numerals on labels, the two fixed dark bands, paper grain.
- **E2E re-baseline** — the deliberate, QA-reviewed rewrite of the automated visitor checks that
  ends the dark-only contract and asserts the light-first system (NB-24 / B38).
