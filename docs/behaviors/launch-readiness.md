# Launch Readiness & Pricing Behaviors — NovaTech AI

> ⚠️ **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED**
> Sections 1–3 (B1–B15: truth & proof integrity, pricing & offers, payment & policy) shipped in
> commit `adc2302` (Phase 1, QA APPROVED). Sections 4–7 (B16–B25) are NOT YET IMPLEMENTED and
> remain the contract for the remaining launch sprint phases.

## 1. Truth & Proof Integrity

- **B1.** Every number, client story, and testimonial published anywhere on the site or in marketing materials must have a real, traceable origin. Claims without a source may not appear.
- **B2.** Until real client work exists, the site must not present any client stories, portfolio items, or aggregate performance statistics. The pilot program (B10) is the only sanctioned source of new proof.
- **B3.** Any system demo shown to visitors must be a real system that actually runs; simulated or mocked demonstrations are prohibited.

## 2. Pricing & Offers

- **B4.** All prices are published in ETB only. Prices appear as anchored starting prices ("from X ETB"), never as open-ended ranges. International engagements are described as "quoted individually" until the business can receive USD.
- **B5.** The Workflow Audit is the fixed entry offer at 85,000 ETB, delivered within 5 business days, and the full fee is credited toward any implementation project that follows.
- **B6.** AI automation is offered in three tiers with starting prices of 250,000 / 750,000 / 1,500,000+ ETB. Each tier publishes the specific scope that raises the price above the starting point.
- **B7.** Monthly support retainers always list what they include (hosting, model usage, monitoring, a defined number of support hours, and a response-time commitment).
- **B8.** Web builds start at 45,000 / 150,000 / 250,000 ETB and mobile builds at 200,000 / 400,000 ETB, matching the published table.
- **B9.** Maintenance is billed monthly at 5,000–15,000 ETB with a written service commitment; annual-charity pricing is prohibited.
- **B10.** The first three implementation clients receive 15–20% off published prices, visibly framed as founding-client rates, in exchange for case study rights, a testimonial, and referral permission.
- **B11.** SaaS products show no prices and are offered only as a waitlist ("in development — tell us you want it") until a real product exists.
- **B12.** Paid advertising services are not offered at launch. If ad campaigns resume, ad spend is always quoted at its actual cost with the management fee stated separately; the spend itself is never discounted.

## 3. Payment & Policy

- **B13.** Until the business license is secured, payment is accepted by bank transfer (CBE Birr) only, and the policy page says so plainly, including the plan to add Telebirr, Chapa, and card payments after licensing.
- **B14.** Development projects follow milestone billing (40% at kickoff, 30% at design approval, 30% before deployment) and the client owns all custom work after final payment.
- **B15.** The site never claims payment capabilities it does not have (e.g., international card handling is not mentioned until it exists).

## 4. Conversion Consistency

- **B16.** The instant estimate wizard and the pricing page must always produce matching numbers for the same scope — the two instruments may never contradict each other.
- **B17.** The estimate wizard's options must not invite scopes below the entry offer; visitors whose needs fall below the entry level are guided to the audit or a discovery call instead.

## 5. Lead Handling

- **B18.** Every inquiry received through the site (quote requests, booking requests, contact forms, guide downloads) is stored so it cannot be silently lost, and is forwarded to the team's working inbox.
- **B19.** Every lead receives a first response within one business day, a discovery call scheduled within three business days, and a proposal within five business days of discovery.

## 6. Design & Voice (the "not AI-generated" standard)

- **B20.** The design uses one desaturated accent color on a neutral base. The dark blue/teal "template" palette, frosted-glass surfaces as the default, equal three-column card rows, and gradient buttons are prohibited.
- **B21.** Cards are used only where visual separation earns its place; elsewhere the layout uses borders, rules, and whitespace.
- **B22.** Typography keeps the current family set (Space Grotesk, Manrope, JetBrains Mono); no generic system font for headings.
- **B23.** Copy must read like a person in Addis wrote it: no filler words (elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen), no formulaic question-answer pairs that all begin the same way, no perfectly parallel headlines on every section, and no placeholder-sounding names, numbers, or quotes. All three languages pass the same standard.
- **B24.** Positioning claims are limited to what is true and provable: founder-delivered work, written agreements, reliable payment integrations, published prices, and response commitments.

## 7. Future State (post-license)

- **B25.** When the license is secured: Telebirr merchant and Chapa payment options are added to the policy and pricing pages, invoice/tax requirements are reflected, and the option to receive USD (for diaspora and international clients) is re-evaluated — at which point the USD column may return with a stated exchange policy.
