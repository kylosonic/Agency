## Public DNS blocker

At release time:

- `novatech.com` resolves to Cloudflare addresses and uses
  `hank.ns.cloudflare.com` and `nellie.ns.cloudflare.com`.
- Public requests redirect to `https://www.novatech.co.uk/`.
- Yegara “My Domains” lists no registered domains.
- Verisign RDAP shows `novatech.com` was first registered in 1998, expires
  2035, and uses Mesh Digital as registrar.
- The Yegara origin certificate does not match `novatech.com`.
- DirectAdmin can edit the origin zone (`ns5.private-nameserver.net` and
  `ns6.private-nameserver.net`) but cannot change the registrar’s public
  nameserver delegation.
- The owner confirmed they do not own or control the existing registration.
- A staged Cloudflare zone was created in the new account with
  `finley.ns.cloudflare.com` and `rosalie.ns.cloudflare.com`. Its apex and
  `www` A records point to `162.244.93.3`; imported mail records were preserved.
  The zone remains pending and does not affect public traffic.

The site cannot become publicly available on `novatech.com` until the domain
owner changes DNS at the domain's registrar or its current Cloudflare zone.
Creating a new Cloudflare account does not grant control of an existing domain.
